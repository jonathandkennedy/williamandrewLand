What a Utah wrongful death claim involves

You do not need to understand any of this today. It is here so you know what the conversation will cover when you are ready to have it.

Who is allowed to bring it

Utah limits a wrongful death claim to certain family members and to the personal representative of the estate. Sorting out who brings it, and on whose behalf, is one of the first things we handle so the family does not have to negotiate it.

The deadline is shorter than for injury

Utah gives less time to file a wrongful death claim than an ordinary injury claim, and far less again when a government vehicle or public entity is involved. This is the one part that genuinely cannot wait.

Evidence still disappears on a timer

If a commercial truck was involved, its electronic data, the driver's logs and any camera footage are subject to the same retention windows as in any other case. Grief does not pause them.

The claim covers more than bills

Funeral and medical expenses, the income and benefits your family has lost, and the loss of the person themselves — their care, their companionship, what they did for the household.

There may be a separate estate claim

What your family member went through between the crash and their death can be a distinct claim brought by the estate, on top of the family's own.

You are not signing anything today

A first conversation is a conversation. Many families call once to understand the deadlines, then call back weeks later. That is completely normal and it is fine.

What we take off your hands

The parts that have deadlines, so your family can deal with the parts that matter.

  1. The insurers stop calling you

    Every adjuster, from every company involved, is redirected to us from the day we are hired. You should not be taking claims calls this week.

  2. We preserve the evidence

    Preservation letters go out immediately — vehicle data, driver records, camera footage, and the vehicles themselves before they are released or repaired.

  3. We handle the estate paperwork

    A wrongful death claim usually needs a personal representative appointed. We do that work rather than sending your family to a second lawyer.

  4. We find everyone responsible

    Not just the driver. The company they worked for, the broker, the maintenance shop, or whoever was responsible for the road.

  5. We deal with the bills

    Medical liens, funeral costs and health-insurer reimbursement claims all arrive at once and are all negotiable. Nobody in your family should be answering those letters.

Straight answers

The questions people actually ask on the first call.

It just happened. Is it too early to call?

No, and there is no pressure attached to calling. Some families want to understand the deadlines and then take weeks before doing anything else. That is a completely reasonable way to handle it. The only genuinely time-sensitive part is preserving evidence, and we can start that without asking anything else of you.

Does our immigration status affect the claim?

No. A family can bring a wrongful death claim whatever their immigration status, and the status of the person who died does not matter either. What we discuss is confidential and we do not report to immigration.

Will our family have to go to court?

Most of these resolve without a trial. Filing a case is not the same as trying one, and much of what happens is paperwork and negotiation your family never has to attend. If it does need a courtroom, you will know long before it gets there.

What does this cost?

Nothing up front and nothing unless there is a recovery. The fee is a percentage agreed in writing beforehand, and case costs come out of the recovery, not out of your family.

Who will we actually be dealing with?

A real person answers the phone, day or night. William Andrews handles these cases personally rather than passing them to a case manager.

Utah generally allows two years from the date of death to file a wrongful death claim — shorter than the four years for an injury claim.
